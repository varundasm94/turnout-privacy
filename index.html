Turnout privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Turnout is a short Android game (package com.mranderson.turnout). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Short version

Turnout does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

The app saves your settings (colour theme, light or dark mode, sound, vibration and display options) in a small file on your phone. This file never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app asks for the vibrate permission only, to give haptic feedback when you play. You can turn this off in the app's settings.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data, and we do not share any data with third parties.

Children

Turnout is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding advertising, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
